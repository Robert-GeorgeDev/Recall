# Data Processing Agreement (DRAFT for lawyer review)

> Not for publication as is. This outlines the Article 28 GDPR content for Octom as processor. A lawyer must review and adapt it before it is offered to customers.

**Parties:** the customer (controller) and [Octom legal entity] (processor).

1. **Subject and duration:** processing of personal data in the customer's workspace to provide Octom, for as long as the customer has an account.
2. **Nature and purpose:** storing, organising and displaying contact records, notes, follow-ups and activity history; generating text suggestions when the customer uses the AI assistant; backups.
3. **Types of data:** names, companies, emails, phone numbers, notes, status and activity about the customer's own contacts and leads. The customer must not store special-category data or card numbers.
4. **Data subjects:** the customer's contacts, leads and clients; the customer's own team members.
5. **Processor obligations:** process only on documented instructions of the customer; confidentiality of personnel; appropriate security measures (see Security page); assist with data subject requests and with security and breach obligations; delete or return data at the end of the service; allow audits within reason.
6. **Subprocessors:** the list on the Subprocessors page. The customer is notified of changes and may object.
7. **International transfers:** only with the safeguards required by GDPR, as described on the Subprocessors page.
8. **Personal data breach:** the processor notifies the customer without undue delay after becoming aware.
9. **Return and deletion:** the customer can export CSV files and delete the account at any time; backups expire on the provider's schedule [state period after verifying with Supabase].
10. **Governing law:** Romanian law. [Lawyer to confirm.]
