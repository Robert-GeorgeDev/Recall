To provide, secure and run Octom we use a limited number of service providers that may process personal data on our behalf.

Depending on their role and the nature of the services provided, these providers are called **subprocessors**.

This page lists the subprocessors Octom uses, the services they are used for, the categories of data that may be processed and the relevant information about international transfers.

We will update this page when we add or remove relevant subprocessors.

## 1. Current subprocessors

The list below shows the providers Octom currently uses that may process personal data in the course of providing the service.

| Provider | Service / purpose | Categories of data that may be processed | Location / transfers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Supabase** | Database, authentication and related infrastructure | Account data, authentication data, workspace data, contacts, notes, follow-ups and activity | Infrastructure configured in the EU, including the Frankfurt region, depending on the service used |
| **Vercel** | Hosting and delivery of the application and of web/API components | Technical data, request information, IP addresses, logs and data needed to provide the service | International provider; some processing may take place outside the EEA |
| **Vercel Web Analytics** | Aggregated analysis of site use, only after the necessary consent | Information about page use and some technical data linked to the visit | International provider; some processing may take place outside the EEA |
| **Stripe** | Payment processing and subscription management | Identification data linked to the payment, email address, information about the plan, subscription, transactions and billing | International provider; some processing may take place outside the EEA |
| **Resend** | Sending operational emails and, if enabled, optional communications | Email addresses and the content of messages sent through the service | International provider; some processing may take place outside the EEA |
| **OpenAI** | Providing artificial intelligence features | Data included in the AI request and the context information sent by Octom to generate the answer | International provider; some processing may take place outside the EEA |

### Supabase

Supabase is used for the application's infrastructure components, including the database and authentication.

Depending on the feature used, the data processed may include account data and the data you enter in the workspace, such as contacts, notes, follow-ups and the activity linked to them.

The Octom project is configured to use infrastructure in the European Union, including the **Frankfurt** region, where the service configuration allows it.

### Vercel

Vercel is used to host and deliver the web application and the related technical components.

In providing the service, technical information about requests to the infrastructure may be processed, including IP addresses, technical identifiers, browser and device information and technical logs.

Some components of Vercel's infrastructure may involve processing data outside the European Economic Area.

### Vercel Web Analytics

Vercel Web Analytics may be used to understand how the site is used.

This service is enabled only after the necessary consent is given.

It may process information about visits and page use, as well as some technical information linked to the visit.

We do not use this service for behavioral advertising.

### Stripe

Stripe is used to process payments and manage paid subscriptions.

Stripe may receive information needed to process the payment, including data linked to the customer, the subscription and the transaction.

Full card details are sent to Stripe and are not stored by Octom.

Stripe may process some data outside the EEA, in accordance with the applicable transfer mechanisms.

### Resend

Resend is used to send emails needed for the service to work, including:

* account emails;
* operational emails;
* periodic summaries, if the user enables this feature;
* messages sent through the contact form.

Depending on the message, the data may include the email address and the content of the message.

### OpenAI

OpenAI is used for certain artificial intelligence features available in Octom.

When you use an AI feature, Octom may send OpenAI the information needed to process the request.

Depending on the feature used, this may include:

* the message sent by the user;
* the name of the selected contact;
* the company;
* the status;
* the notes;
* recent activity;
* a limited conversation context, where needed to generate the answer (for example, the most recent messages).

OpenAI may process this data outside the EEA, including in the United States.

Octom does not intentionally send OpenAI information that is not needed for the AI feature used.

However, the content sent may contain personal data entered or selected by the user.

For more information about the use of AI features, see the **Privacy policy** and the **Terms and conditions**.

## 2. How we select subprocessors

When we select providers that may have access to personal data, we consider, as far as reasonable and applicable:

* whether the service is necessary for Octom to work;
* the security measures implemented;
* the provider's ability to meet the applicable data protection requirements;
* the contractual mechanisms available;
* the locations where data may be processed;
* the legal mechanisms available for international transfers.

Subprocessors are used only as far as needed to provide the services they were engaged for.

## 3. Transfers outside the EEA

Some of our providers are established outside the European Economic Area or use infrastructure that may involve the transfer of, or access to, data from outside the EEA.

When such a transfer is subject to the GDPR, we rely on a legal transfer mechanism recognized by applicable law.

Depending on the provider and the circumstances of the transfer, this may include:

* an **adequacy decision** of the European Commission;
* the **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)** approved by the European Commission;
* other legal mechanisms available under the GDPR.

For transfers based on SCCs or a similar mechanism, additional measures may be applied where they are necessary given the circumstances of the transfer.

The specific information about the transfer mechanisms that apply to each provider may be updated as the infrastructure and services used by Octom evolve.

## 4. Changes to the list of subprocessors

We may add or remove subprocessors when this is necessary to provide or improve the service.

We will update this page when the list of subprocessors changes in a relevant way.

For customers who have entered into a **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)** with Octom, the notification and the procedure for objecting to a new subprocessor will be governed, as applicable, by the DPA.

If an applicable DPA gives the customer the right to object to the addition of a subprocessor, we will follow the procedure and deadlines set out in that agreement.

The date shown at the top of this page indicates the last update of the list.
