This page explains how paid plans, billing, subscription cancellation and refunds work for the Octom service.

This policy is part of the **Octom Terms and conditions**. If it conflicts with a mandatory legal right, the mandatory legal right applies.

## 1. Plans and prices

Octom may offer free plans and paid plans.

**During the launch period, Octom is free and paid subscriptions are not available.** No payments are taken, and the information below will apply only if and when we introduce paid plans.

The indicative prices we have in mind are:

* **Free** – no cost;
* **Pro** – **EUR 7.99/month**;
* **Business** – **EUR 14.99/month**.

The final price and the limits of each plan will be shown on the site and in the application before you subscribe. You will not be moved to a paid plan without your express agreement.

Before you pay, you will see the relevant information about:

* the plan selected;
* the price;
* the billing period;
* whether the payment is recurring, where applicable;
* the currency and any applicable taxes (including VAT, where applicable), as far as they must be shown under the law.

Payments are processed through **Stripe**.

Octom does not store the full number of the bank card used for payment.

## 2. How billing works

Paid plans are currently billed **monthly**.

When you subscribe to a paid plan, you are charged for the first billing period at the time of purchase, unless the order process indicates otherwise.

The subscription period starts on the date the paid plan is activated.

For a monthly subscription, the next payment will be made at the start of the next billing period, if the subscription has not been cancelled.

At present Octom does not offer a free trial period for paid plans, unless a special offer expressly says otherwise.

## 3. Automatic renewal

Paid subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period until they are cancelled.

By buying a recurring subscription, you authorize the payment processor to make the recurring payments for the subscription, under the conditions shown at the time of purchase.

If a renewal payment fails, we may:

* try to process the payment again;
* ask you to update the payment method;
* temporarily limit access to the paid plan's features;
* move the account to a plan available free of charge;
* suspend or close the subscription, in accordance with the applicable terms.

We will not treat a failed payment as an automatic cancellation of the subscription unless the billing flow expressly provides so.

## 4. Cancelling the subscription

You can cancel the subscription at any time from the **"Manage subscription"** feature in your account or by contacting us at **[contact@octom.eu](mailto:contact@octom.eu)**.

When you cancel a subscription:

* automatic renewal is stopped;
* no payments will be made for future periods;
* access to the paid plan normally continues until the end of the period already paid.

**Cancelling the subscription does not automatically mean deleting your account or data.**

If you want to delete the account and the associated data, you must use the account deletion feature, if available, or contact us.

Data is handled in accordance with the **Privacy policy** and the retention periods set by law.

## 5. Refunds

If you cancel a subscription, the cancellation stops future payments but does not automatically generate a refund for the period already paid.

For users who do not have a legal right to a refund, payments for the subscription period already started are, in principle, non-refundable.

However, this rule **does not limit or exclude any legal refund rights** you may have.

A refund may be due, for example, when:

* the law gives you a right of withdrawal and the applicable conditions are met;
* the digital service is not supplied in line with the applicable legal obligations;
* the service has a lack of conformity for which the law gives you the right to a price reduction or refund;
* you were charged because of a technical error;
* the same period was billed more than once;
* Octom confirms, exceptionally, that a refund is justified.

When a refund is approved, it will be made, where possible, using the same payment method as the original transaction.

The actual processing of the refund may depend on the time needed by the payment processor and the card issuer.

### Requesting a refund

If you think you were charged incorrectly or that you are entitled to a refund, contact us at:

**[contact@octom.eu](mailto:contact@octom.eu)**

If possible, include:

* the email address linked to the account;
* the payment date;
* the amount paid;
* the reason for the request.

Do not send us the full card number or other sensitive bank details by email.

We will review the request and tell you the outcome in accordance with applicable law.

## 6. The consumer right of withdrawal

If you use Octom as a **consumer**, you may benefit from the legal right to withdraw from the contract within the period and under the conditions set by applicable law.

For distance contracts, consumer protection law provides, in principle, a period of **14 days** to exercise the right of withdrawal.

There are, however, special rules when the supply of a digital service starts before the withdrawal period ends.

If you ask for the service to start before the withdrawal period ends, the legal conditions on proportionate payment for the services supplied may apply or, for certain forms of digital content, the right of withdrawal may be lost once the legal conditions are met.

Before you complete such a purchase, we will give you the information and options required by applicable law.

Nothing in this policy excludes or limits mandatory consumer rights.

## 7. Billing problems

If you notice a problem with a payment or an invoice, contact us as soon as possible at:

**[contact@octom.eu](mailto:contact@octom.eu)**

Among other things, we can investigate:

* duplicate payments;
* payments for a cancelled subscription;
* differences between the price shown and the amount billed;
* failed payments;
* problems with activating or deactivating the subscription;
* other billing errors.

If the problem is caused by the payment processor, we may need to work with it to resolve the situation.

## 8. Changing prices

We may change the prices of Octom plans for the future.

Changes do not affect payments already made for subscription periods already paid.

If you have an active subscription and its price is about to change, we will tell you before the new price applies to the next billing period, as far as required by applicable law.

If you do not want to continue the subscription at the new price, you can cancel it before the change takes effect.
