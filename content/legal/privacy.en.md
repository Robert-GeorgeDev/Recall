## 2. What personal data we process

Depending on how you use Octom, we may process the following categories of data:

### 2.1. Account data

To create and manage your account we may process:

* your email address;
* technical account identifiers;
* the information needed for authentication;
* preferences linked to your account, such as language or some service settings.

Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are not accessible to Octom staff in readable form.

### 2.2. Workspace data

If you use Octom to manage contacts and follow-up activities, you may enter information such as:

* first and last name;
* company or organization;
* email address;
* phone number;
* statuses and labels;
* notes;
* dates and information about follow-ups;
* activity history;
* any other information you choose to enter in the workspace.

The content you enter may contain personal data about you and about other people.

### 2.3. Data about the use of AI features

When you use Octom's AI features, we process the data needed to carry out the request and, where applicable, technical and usage information needed for security, abuse prevention and applying usage limits.

Depending on the feature, the request sent to the AI service may include information you selected in Octom, such as the name, company, status, notes or recent activity of a contact.

### 2.4. Team and collaboration data

If you use the team features, we may process:

* the email addresses of invited members;
* information about membership of a workspace;
* their role and permissions;
* relevant activity carried out in the workspace.

Members of a workspace may have access to the information and data to which the workspace administrator has given them permissions.

### 2.5. Communication preferences

We may process information about your preferences for:

* operational emails;
* periodic summaries;
* product communications;
* commercial communications and offers.

For commercial communications that require consent, we also keep the information needed to show that consent was given or withdrawn.

### 2.6. Subscription and payment data

If you choose a paid plan, we may process information such as:

* the plan chosen;
* the subscription status;
* the customer or subscription identifier;
* information about transactions and invoices;
* data needed to administer the subscription.

Card payments are processed through **Stripe**. Octom does not receive or store the full card number.

### 2.7. Technical data

For the operation, security and diagnosis of the service we may process certain technical data, such as:

* IP address;
* browser and device information;
* technical identifiers;
* data about access to and use of the service;
* information about errors and technical events.

Depending on the technology used and your settings, some technical data may be collected through cookies or similar technologies. For more information, see **Cookies and browser storage**.

### 2.8. Data you should not enter

Octom is not designed to store special categories of data or extremely sensitive information.

We do not ask you to enter into the service:

* complete bank details or banking passwords;
* passwords for other services;
* medical data;
* bank card information;
* passwords or credentials that are not needed to use Octom.

If you choose to enter such information in free-text fields, you do so at your own responsibility, and Octom is not designed to handle such information.

We process personal data only where we have an applicable legal basis.

We process the data needed for:

* creating and managing the account;

* providing the AI features you request;

The legal basis is, as applicable, **performance of a contract** or taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, under Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

We may process certain data for:

* protecting accounts;
* detecting and preventing misuse;
* preventing fraud;
* applying usage limits;
* investigating incidents;
* maintaining the security and integrity of the infrastructure.

The legal basis is, as applicable, **our legitimate interest**, under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

### 3.3. Legal obligations

We may process and keep certain information where necessary to comply with legal obligations, including in accounting and tax matters, or to respond to legal requests from competent authorities.

The legal basis is **compliance with a legal obligation**, under Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.

If you choose "Accept" in the cookie banner, we use Vercel Web Analytics to understand how the site is used. The legal basis is **your consent**, under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. You can decline or withdraw consent at any time from "Cookie settings" in the site footer.

### 3.5. Commercial communications

Where the law requires consent, we will ask for your consent before sending commercial communications.

You can withdraw your consent at any time, using the unsubscribe link in emails or by contacting us, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

Some communications that are strictly necessary for the operation of the service, for example messages about your account, security or important changes to the service, may be sent regardless of consent to commercial communications.

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes for which it was collected or as long as needed to meet legal obligations.

* some data may be kept for a limited period after the account is closed, where necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or legal obligations;
* billing data and financial and accounting documents are kept for the period set by applicable law;
* data in backups may remain temporarily in the backup copies until the normal retention cycle ends;

* **right to erasure** – you can ask for your data to be deleted under the conditions set by law;
* **right to restriction of processing** – you can ask us to limit how we use your data in certain situations;
* **right to data portability** – in the cases set by the GDPR, you can receive certain data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format;
* **right to object** – you can object to certain processing, including processing based on legitimate interest;
* **right to withdraw consent** – where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time;
* **right to lodge a complaint** with the competent data protection authority.
