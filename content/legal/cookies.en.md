This page explains how Octom uses cookies, local storage and similar technologies to make the service work and, if you agree, to analyze how it is used.

We aim to use as little browser storage as possible and we do not use cookies for behavioral advertising.

## 1. What cookies and browser storage are

Cookies are small files or values that a website can store in your device's browser.

Octom may also use browser storage mechanisms such as **localStorage** or similar technologies.

In this policy we use the term "browser storage" to refer, in general, to cookies and these similar technologies.

Some of these technologies are strictly necessary for Octom to work, while others are used only if you give your consent.

## 2. What we store and why

### 2.1. Login session

Octom uses storage mechanisms that are necessary to keep your login session.

They let the application recognize that you are logged in and give you access to your account and workspace.

Without them, login and secure access to the account could not work normally.

They are used for the operation and security of the service and are not used by Octom for behavioral advertising.

### 2.2. Language preference

We may store your preference for the interface language, for example Romanian or English.

This information is used to display the interface in the language you chose.

### 2.3. Selected plan

If you choose a plan before finishing account creation, we may keep the selected plan locally and temporarily.

At present this information is kept for a maximum of **24 hours** and is used to continue the sign-up and payment process.

This information is not used for advertising or to track your activity on other websites.

### 2.4. Cookie preference

If we show you a consent banner for cookies and similar technologies, we may store your choice so that we do not ask you for the same option repeatedly.

For example, we may use the identifier:

`octom-consent`

This identifier is used to remember your preference for optional technologies.

## 3. Optional analytics

Octom may use **Vercel Web Analytics** to understand how the site is used and to improve the service.

Analytics is **optional** and is enabled only after you give consent through our cookie and similar technologies mechanism.

If you choose **"Decline"**, the analytics tool is not loaded.

In the current configuration we do not use advertising cookies and we do not use analytics to track your activity on third-party sites for advertising purposes.

Depending on how the service is configured and on the technologies used by the provider, some technical information about the use of the site may be sent to the analytics provider.

For more information about the processing of personal data, see the **Privacy policy**.

### Consent for analytics

We do not load the analytics service before consent is given where consent is required by applicable law.

You can decline analytics without it affecting your access to Octom's main features.

## 4. Your choices and consent

When we use technologies that require your consent, we give you the option to:

* accept the optional technologies;
* decline the optional technologies;
* change your choice later.

Declining optional technologies does not affect the use of the main features of the service, except where a technology is necessary for that feature to work.

Your preference is kept in the browser so that your choice can be applied.

You can change your preferences at any time with the **"Cookie settings"** option in the site footer.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

## 5. Deleting and blocking storage

You can delete or block cookies and other locally stored data using your browser settings.

Data stored by Octom can also be deleted through the settings available in your browser.

If you delete the data needed for login, you may be logged out of your account.

If you delete local preferences, it is possible that:

* the chosen language is reset;
* the cookie preference is asked for again;
* the temporarily selected plan is no longer available locally;
* the login session is closed.

Disabling all forms of browser storage may affect the operation of some Octom components.

## 6. If something changes

We may change the technologies Octom uses to improve the service, security, performance or features.

If we introduce new cookies or similar technologies that require your consent, they will not be activated before the necessary consent is obtained.

We will update this page when changes are relevant to how we use cookies and similar technologies.

**The date shown at the top of this page indicates the last update.**
