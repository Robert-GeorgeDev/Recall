# Code of conduct

Octom is a small project and we want it to stay a calm place to work.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, never with people.
- Be patient with beginners and with people who write in a second language.
- Keep feedback specific and kind. Say what you need, not only what is wrong.
- Respect privacy. Do not share other people's personal data.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Spam, including promotion that has nothing to do with the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break
these rules, and may block people who do so repeatedly. To report a problem,
write to the contact address listed on the repository profile. Reports are
handled privately.

This code of conduct applies in the repository and in any space where people
represent the project.
